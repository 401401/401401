# Ali Beiranvand 👋

### 🎓 Ph.D. Student in Electronics Engineering | Lorestan University
### Speech Signal Processing & Deep Learning Researcher

I am a Ph.D. student in Electronics Engineering at Lorestan University, with a dedicated research focus on **Speech and Audio Signal Processing**. My research bridges the gap between classical Digital Signal Processing (DSP) and contemporary Deep Learning paradigms. I specialize in developing robust algorithms for real-world speech applications, including Noise Cancellation, Emotion Recognition, and Speech Separation.

---

### 🔬 Research Interests & Expertise

**🎙️ Speech Enhancement & Noise Cancellation**
- **Classical DSP Approaches:** Wiener Filtering, Spectral Subtraction, and Adaptive Line Enhancer (ALE).
- **Deep Learning Approaches:** PSO-optimized Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for robust noise suppression.

**🧠 Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)**
- Development of a noise-robust SER framework utilizing a hybrid approach of **Data Augmentation**, Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (**BiLSTM**) networks, and **Random Forest (RF)** classifiers.

**🗣️ Speech Separation (Current Research)**
- Implementation of the **Conv-TasNet** architecture (Time-domain, Single-channel).
- Integration of **Channel Attention** mechanisms to enhance separation performance.
